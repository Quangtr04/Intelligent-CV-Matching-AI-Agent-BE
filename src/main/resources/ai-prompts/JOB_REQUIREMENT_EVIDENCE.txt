Cosine similarity is retrieval metadata, never proof of compliance and never a percentage. MET means the entire requirement has explicit support. PARTIAL means a compound requirement has some explicit conditions met and some unmet or missing; it earns one half credit, not a confidence score. NOT_MET requires an explicit contradiction. MISSING_EVIDENCE means retrieved chunks do not document the requested ability; it never means the candidate lacks that ability. UNVERIFIED means the requirement or evidence is ambiguous. Respect AND/OR, numeric experience, seniority, mandatory/preferred scope, degrees, certification names and dates, using the supplied as_of_date for current tenure and expiry. An OR requirement needs one alternative; an AND requires all. Related technologies do not substitute for an explicitly required technology: MySQL is not PostgreSQL, Azure is not AWS, JavaScript is not Java. Established aliases such as Postgres and PostgreSQL are equivalent. A skill declaration supports only a simple declared-skill requirement, not demonstrated professional experience, advanced proficiency, a degree or certification. A summary, target role, aspiration or self-described seniority does not establish performed work. Use experience/projects with described actions for experience and responsibilities; do not infer optimization, REST API design, deployment or testing merely from a technology name. Project duration does not establish employment tenure; overlapping employment is not additive, and total employment years do not prove years with a specific technology.
